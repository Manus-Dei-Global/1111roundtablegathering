The Convergence

The 11:11 Roundtable Gathering

A strategic convening of kingdom voices, called to fellowship, dialogue, alignment and collaboration for the advancement of God's purposes.

✦

The Mandate

To sound a prophetic call for revival and order in the Body of Christ, awakening the Church to her identity and governmental responsibility as a kingdom of priests, enthroning Christ in every sphere, and establishing the government and order of God upon the earth. This mandate calls the Church to return to divine order, arise in her priestly and kingly identity, and step into the fullness of her expression as the Body of Christ.

◇

The Mission

To convene and connect Ministers of Christ, Marketplace Apostles, Kingdom Ambassadors, Visionaries, and Pioneers across diverse spheres, creating a strategic platform for fellowship, dialogue, alignment, collaboration, and the advancement of God's purposes for His Church. Through the 11:11 Roundtable Gathering, we seek to bring key voices and kingdom leaders together, facilitate meaningful conversations around the issues confronting the Body of Christ, and communicate the prophetic message and mandate for the season. We believe that when the leadership is aligned, the Church can be mobilised; and when the Church is united and ordered, her influence can reach the nations.

✧

The Vision

To see a united and ordered Body of Christ, fully awakened to her identity, authority, responsibility, and governmental expression, functioning together according to the pattern of Christ. The vision is to see the Church move beyond fragmentation into unity, maturity, order, and collective expression, so that Christ may be fully manifested through His Body and His government established on the earth.

Who we are

About MDGIFT

Manus Dei Global Initiative for Transformation (MDGIFT) is an institutional organization with a transgenerational mandate to bring about transformation and revival, bringing spiritual, personal, and societal transformation and revival by aligning lives, systems, and structures with God's divine order. Anchored on the scriptural foundation of Exodus 25:8–9, MDGIFT seeks to build according to God's blueprint, restore order, and raise a Kingdom standard through diverse initiatives.

“Have the people of Israel build me a holy sanctuary so I can live among them. You must build this Tabernacle and its furnishings exactly like the pattern I will show you.”Exodus 25:8–9 · NLT
Our institutional foundation

Framework

01

Mandate
Transformation & Revival

To bring about spiritual, personal, and societal transformation and revival by aligning lives, systems, and structures with God's divine order.

02

Mission
Divine Service to Humanity

To serve humanity through initiatives that communicate the love, truth, presence, and purposes of God, providing spiritual, social, practical, and transformational solutions to human needs.

To see humanity restored to God's divine order, awakened through revival, and brought into the fullness of His purpose, resulting in the redemption and transformation of individuals, communities, systems, and society.

One mandate, many expressions

Our Initiative

Six expressions of service, formation and transformation under the MDGIFT vision.

✟

Manus Dei Christian Centre

Building According to Pattern

A nurturing centre committed to transforming lives and reviving souls through discipleship, ministry, training, consultation, fellowship, and practical expressions of the Christian faith.

Manus Dei Clinic · Manus Dei MINISTRY · Manus Dei UNITED · Kingdom Training Institute · Manus Dei FOUNDATION
♡

Project I CAN

A Simple CAN Can Make a Change.

A micro-giving initiative that turns small contributions into immediate acts of compassion for people in need. Small acts of generosity become meaningful expressions of love, care, and practical support.

☼

Mission Hope & Light

Tripartite Prosperity

A Christ-centred initiative awakening lives through the light of Christ and guiding individuals into true prosperity, beginning with the spirit and extending into every area of life. Transformation begins with salvation and must impact the spirit, soul, and body.

⇄

BRiDGE

Bridging the Gap Between the Church and the Unchurched

An outreach initiative taking the Gospel beyond traditional church settings, reaching the unchurched through intentional presence, practical support, and compassionate engagement.

⌂

Bank of Welfare

Relief – Stability – Sustainable

A community-driven initiative providing relief and welfare, building pathways to stability and sustainability: from crisis to capability, from dependence to contribution.

♔

Priesthood Assembly

Called. Chosen. Consecrated.

A sacred gathering of the called and chosen, consecrated as vessels to incubate God's glory and advance His Kingdom. Rooted in 1 Peter 2:9 and Revelation 5:10, carrying God's presence and ruling in the earth as priests and kings.
